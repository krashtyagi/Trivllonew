**a. Information You Provide**

- Name, email address, phone number, and date of birth when you register or make a booking.

- Travel preferences, special requests, and feedback you share with us.

## 4\. ABOUT US

### Who We Are

Trivllo is a modern travel platform built for the way people explore today — fast, flexible, and full of possibilities.

We bring together hotels, tours, and experiences into one seamless platform, making it easier for travelers to plan, book, and enjoy everything their destination has to offer — without jumping between apps or losing hours to research.

---

### Our Story

Trivllo was born from a simple frustration: travel planning was too scattered, too complicated, and too time-consuming.

We believed there was a better way. A single place where you could find a great hotel, book a guided tour, and discover local activities — all in one go, with the confidence that everything is verified and the process is smooth.

So we built it.

Today, Trivllo serves travelers across India, helping them discover stays and experiences that match their style, budget, and curiosity.

---

### Our Mission

To make travel accessible, enjoyable, and effortless for every kind of traveler — whether you're planning a weekend getaway, a family holiday, or a solo adventure.

---

### Our Vision

A world where discovering and booking the perfect travel experience is as easy as a few taps — and where every trip becomes a story worth telling.
